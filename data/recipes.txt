PEANUT BUTTER COOKIES
Category: dessert
Prep: 15 min | Cook: 15 min | Oven: 350°F | Serves: 4 x 12

Ingredients:
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup creamy peanut butter
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 large eggs, room temp
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp water
- 2 1/4 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt & 1 tsp baking soda

Directions:
1. Cream together butter, peanut butter, and sugars.
2. Add eggs one at a time.
3. Beat in vanilla & water.
4. In separate bowl, combine remaining dry ingredients.
5. Mix together both mixtures.
6. Drop by Tbsp & flatten with fork.
7. Bake at 350°F, 11-14 min.

BAKED PENNE PASTA
Category: entree
(From the "grandma house cookbook")

Ingredients:
- 4-6 sausage links, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 large any-color bell peppers (chop large)
- 1 large red onion, chopped
- 2-4 Tbsp cooking wine/grape juice
- 1 can canned tomatoes, 28 oz (794 g)
- 1 can tomato puree, 15 oz (425 g)
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 lb penne pasta (454 g)
- 8 oz mozzarella, cut in small chunks (226 g)
- 1/2 c. grated parmesan (or cheese that melts)

Directions:
Brown sausage in skillet over medium heat (you can also use ground sausage). Drain any fat. Add peppers, onion & garlic. Saute 8 minutes. Add wine/juice. Add tomatoes, puree, salt & pepper, breaking up tomatoes with spoon. Reduce heat to low. Cover, simmer 10 minutes. Preheat oven to 400°F/204°C. Boil pasta according to directions. Drain pasta, add tomato mixture, add mozzarella and mix well. Pour into a sprayed 9x13" dish and top with Parmesan or a white cheese that melts. Bake 25 minutes or till bubbly.

BEEF STROGANOFF
Category: entree
(Worcestershire sub: 1/2 Tbsp ketchup, 1/2 Tbsp vinegar, 1/4 Tbsp soy sauce)

Ingredients:
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 medium onion, diced
- 1 lb lean ground beef
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 Tbsp flour
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 cup whipping cream
- 1/3 cup sour cream
- 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 tsp salt, adjust to taste
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper

Directions:
Dice onion in tiny bits, then saute in olive oil till clear. Add ground beef and break up with spatula as you cook till well done. Add minced garlic and saute with beef for 4 more minutes. In a small mixing bowl, whisk flour and cool beef broth (can use Maggi). Add to bowl, cream/s, Worcestershire, salt & pepper. Mix till all incorporated and add to beef. Heat all till bubbly. Garnish with chopped parsley (opt). And serve over cooked pasta or rice.

APPLE CRISP
Category: dessert
6 servings; 295 calories per serving

Ingredients:
- 4 cups sliced tart apples (about 4 medium)
- 2/3 to 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour*
- 1/2 cup oats
- 1/3 cup margarine or butter, softened
- 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 3/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg

Directions:
Heat oven to 375°F. Arrange apples in greased square pan, 8x8x2 inches. Mix remaining ingredients; sprinkle over apples.
Bake until topping is golden brown and apples are tender, about 30 minutes. Serve warm and, if desired, with cream or ice cream.
*Self-rising flour can be used in this recipe.

Variations:
- Apricot Crisp: Substitute 2 cans (17 oz each) apricot halves, drained, for the apples, and use the lesser amount of brown sugar.
- Cherry Crisp: Substitute 1 can (21 oz) cherry pie filling for the apples, and use the lesser amount of brown sugar.
- Peach Crisp: Substitute 1 can (29 oz) sliced peaches, drained, for the apples, and use the lesser amount of brown sugar.
- Pineapple Crisp: Substitute 2 cans (13 1/2 oz each) pineapple chunks, drained, or 2 cans (20 oz each) crushed pineapple, drained, for the apples, and use the lesser amount of brown sugar.

CREAM PUFFS
Category: dessert

Puffs:
- 1 C. water
- 1/2 C. butter
- 1 C. flour
- 4 eggs
Boil water & butter. Add flour. Cook till it forms a ball. Add eggs 1 at a time. Mix very well after each egg. With spoon form into 2-inch diameter balls, 2 inches apart. Bake at 400°F for 40-45 minutes.

Filling:

- 1/2 C. flour
- Little salt
- 2 egg yolks
- 2 C. scalded milk
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 C. whipped cream
Mix dry, add to milk, cook 2-3 min. Add yolk, cook 2-3 min. Cool. Add vanilla & whipped cream.

SCALLOPED POTATOES
Category: side

Ingredients:
- 1 quart heavy cream (a later copy of this recipe crosses out "2 quarts" and writes 1)
- 2 tablespoons onion powder
- 1 tablespoon chicken stock base
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 3 cups fontina cheese / white cheddar (hand shredded)
- 6.5 potatoes, skinned and cleaned
- Tabasco, 3 Tbsp

Instructions:
1. Slice potatoes super thin and put in water.
2. Mix onion, garlic, base, cream, Tabasco.
3. Spray your pan very well.
4. Put a bottom layer of cream mixture and cheese to cover the pan.
5. Layer potatoes in a weave.
6. Every two layers put more cream mixture and cheese.
7. Finish top with leftover cheese and cream mixture.
8. Bake at 325°F covered for 1.5 hrs or until potatoes are soft in the middle. (Original said 450°F; the updated copy corrects it to 325°F.)
9. Uncover & bake for 30 more minutes. (Handwritten addition on the updated copy.)

To serve: put potatoes in an oven-safe bowl, cover the top with cheese and put in oven on broil on high till cheese is melted.

ZUCCHINI BREAD
Category: bread

Ingredients:
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup canola oil
- 3 large eggs, room temperature
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder

WHOLE WHEAT ZUCCHINI BREAD
Category: bread

Ingredients:
- 3 beaten eggs
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 2 cups brown sugar, firmly packed
- 2 cups ground zucchini
- 2 tablespoons vanilla extract
- 3 cups whole wheat flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 3 teaspoons cinnamon
- 1 cup chopped nuts

Directions:
Mix together eggs, oil, brown sugar, zucchini and vanilla extract in a large bowl.
In a separate bowl, combine flour, baking soda, salt, baking powder and cinnamon. Add flour mixture to zucchini mixture, stir until smooth. Fold in nuts.
Pour batter into large loaf pan, or two smaller ones.
Bake at 325°F for 75 minutes, or until a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean.

WHOLE WHEAT ZUCCHINI BREAD (WITH HONEY)
Category: bread

Ingredients:
- 3 eggs
- 2 C. zucchini*
- 1 1/2 C. honey
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 3 tsp. cinnamon
- 1 C. chopped walnuts
- 2 tsp. vanilla

CHICKEN TETRAZZINI

KING RANCH CHICKEN
Category: entree

Ingredients:
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 16 oz sour cream
- 1/2 C. picante sauce
- 1 boiled chicken with broth
- 2 Tbsp. chili powder
- 3 Tbsp garlic powder
- 1-2 chicken bouillon cubes
- 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- Tortilla chips

Directions:
Debone chicken, put in pan with a cup of broth, chili powder, garlic powder & bouillon. Let broth boil off. Make sauce by adding to a bowl: cream of chicken soup, sour cream & picante sauce. In pan layer sauce, chips, chicken and cheese in that order. Bake at 350°F for about 1 hour.

1910 GREAT GRANDMOTHER CHERRY CAKE (low-carb version)
Category: dessert
Recipe by Raschel Sadler

Ingredients:
- 1/2 C. butter or coconut oil
- 1/2 C. erythritol
- 1/2 (C.) Splenda or stevia
- 5 eggs
- 2 tsp. almond extract
- 2 C. almond flour/meal
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1/4 C. chopped, no-sugar-added dried cherries (or cranberries)
- 1/4 C. chopped SF (sugar-free) chocolate chips

Directions:
Mix first 5 ingredients together. Pour almond flour atop the wet mixture. Stir the baking powder just into the top of the almond flour a little, then mix it all together with the wet mixture. Chop up the cherries & chocolate chips with a sharp knife or chopper and stir into the cake mixture (pecans may be added if desired). Pour mixture into a bread pan lined with foil. Bake at 350°F for 50 minutes.

Carb notes (sticky note):
- Erythritol: 24 carbs for 1/2 C.
- Splenda: 12 carbs for 1/2 C.
- Almond flour: 48 carbs for 2 C.
- Cherries: 36 carbs for 1/4 C. (sweetened)
- Chocolate chips: 34 carbs for 1/4 + 1/8 C.
= 154 carbs for the whole thing

Carb notes (second card, "Great Grandmother Cake"):
- Erythritol: 96 carbs
- Splenda: 12 carbs
- Eggs: 3 carbs
- Flour (almond): 32 carbs
- Stevia chocolate chips: 25.14 carbs
- Cherries: 46.5 carbs
Total: 215 carbs / 12 = 18 carbs per slice

NANAIMO BARS
Category: dessert

Bottom layer:
- 1/2 C. butter
- 1/4 C. sugar
- 5 T. cocoa
- 1 egg
- 1 3/4 C. graham cracker crumbs
- 3/4 C. coconut
- 1/2 C. finely chopped walnuts
Melt first 3 ingredients in HEAVY saucepan. Add beaten egg and stir. Remove from heat. Stir in crumbs, coconut, and nuts. Press firmly in ungreased 9x9-in pan.

2nd layer:
- 1/2 C. butter
- 3 Tbsp milk
- 2 T. custard powder (vanilla)
- 2 C. icing sugar
Beat all ingredients till light. Spread over bottom layer.

3rd layer:
- 2/3 C. semisweet chocolate chips
- 2 T. butter
Melt chips & butter over low heat. When cool (not hard), spread over 2nd layer. Chill completely in fridge. Cut with sharp knife.

Note on custard powder (from a message): "Vanilla custard powder" is essentially vanilla-flavored corn starch, but using just corn starch turns out a bit weird. Instead use 1.5 Tbsp cook-and-serve vanilla pudding powder + 1/2 Tbsp corn starch. Any nut works in the crust — pecans, walnuts, or almonds.

MICROWAVE CARAMEL CORN
Category: snack

Ingredients:
- 2 pitchers of popped corn
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/4 cup corn syrup (Karo)
- A little salt
- 1 paper bag

Directions:
1. Bring all ingredients to a boil (except for popcorn).
2. Cook 1 minute longer.
3. Put popcorn in bag and pour caramel over it.
4. Fold top of bag and shake for 1 minute (bag will get hot).
5. Put bag in microwave for 2 minutes on HIGH.
6. Remove and shake for 1 minute.
7. Put bag back in microwave for 2 minutes on HIGH.
8. Remove and shake for 1 minute.
9. Pour out on clean table and let cool (VERY HOT!!!!)

DOUBLE FUDGE BROWNIES (Grandma's)
Category: dessert
Recipe by Joyce Hanlon | Yield: 24

Ingredients:
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 3 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup pecans, or more if desired
- 6 oz semisweet chocolate (1 cup choc. chips)
- 6 tablespoons butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs

Directions:
Preheat oven to 350°F and grease & flour an 8- or 9-inch square pan. Sift flour, cocoa, baking soda, and salt. Coarsely chop pecans. Melt butter and chocolate over low heat, stirring till all chocolate is melted, & remove from heat. Stir in the sugar & vanilla. Cool mixture for 10 minutes. Whisk in eggs and beat batter till all incorporated. Stir in the flour mixture and then the nuts and mix only until the batter is just blended. Spread batter evenly in prepared pan. Bake on the middle rack in the oven for 25-30 minutes. Keep in pan for at least 2 hours before cutting.
(Picture on card was baked in an 8x8 pan.)

- 2 tsp white sugar
- 2 C. warm water (almost hot)
- 4 Tbsp oil
- 2 tsp salt
- 5 C. flour

TUSCAN CHICKEN
Category: entree

Ingredients:
- Pam or other spray oil
- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- Salt
- Black pepper
- 1 tsp. dried oregano (*or herb mix)
- 3 tbsp. olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, minced (jarred is fine)
- 3 c. cherry tomatoes, halved (or 6 romas sliced long)
- 6 c. baby spinach (almost a full 6 oz bag)
- 1.5 c. heavy cream
- 1/2 c. Parmesan cheese (powdered style okay)

Directions:
Spray a skillet with Pam & heat over medium-high. Add chicken seasoned with salt, pepper, and oregano. Cook until golden and no longer pink, 8 minutes per side. Remove & set chicken to the side. (You can also cut raw chicken into medallion-sized pieces before cooking.)
In skillet used for the chicken, heat oil & stir in garlic & tomatoes seasoned with salt and pepper. Cook about 5 minutes. Add heavy cream and Parmesan and bring mixture to a simmer. BRAIDED CHEESE BREAD
Category: bread
Yield: 4 loaves | Recipe by Joyce Hanlon

Dough:
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/3 cup powdered milk
- 2 eggs
- 2 tablespoons vanilla
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 3/4 cup water (plus 1/2 cup warm water for the yeast)
- 3/4 tablespoon salt
- 2 tablespoons yeast
- 6 to 7 cups flour

Cream cheese filling:
- 2 (8 oz) pkgs. cream cheese, softened
- 2 eggs (printed "1 egg", hand-corrected to 2)
- 1/4 cup sugar

Icing:
- 4 cups powdered sugar
- 1/4 cup butter
- Milk to thin

Directions:
Melt butter in 1 3/4 cup of water, set aside to cool. In mixing bowl, add 2 Tbsp of yeast, 1 Tbsp sugar, and 1/2 cup of warm water. Let set till it proofs. After your yeast mixture proofs, add the cooled water/butter mixture, 1/2 cup of sugar, 3/4 Tbsp salt, 1/3 cup of powdered milk, 2 eggs and 2 Tbsp of vanilla. Mix well with mixer. Add rest of flour and mix by hand till dough comes away from sides. Knead on lightly floured surface for about 10 minutes. Form ball and put in greased bowl. Cover with plastic wrap and set in warm place till double in size. Punch down and let rest for about 5 minutes. Divide dough into 4 balls. Cover each with plastic wrap to keep from drying out. In a small mixing bowl mix cream cheese, egg and 1/4 cup sugar together and set aside for the "cream cheese filling".

Assembling: Divide cream cheese mixture evenly into 4 different bowls or ziplock bags. Unwrap 1st ball. Shape it into a "log". Roll out to a 13x5-inch rectangle. Carefully tie a knot with these 2 center strips. Place on a lined cookie sheet (2 loaves will fit on one standard cookie sheet). Let the braided loaves rise for 20 minutes then bake at 350°F for 20 minutes or till golden brown. While those 2 loaves are baking, prepare the other 2 loaves. Mix up icing in small mixing bowl. You want the icing only thin enough to be able to drizzle it over the loaf. Ice while still warm.

If desired, raspberries or cherries, or a pie filling, can be added on top of the cream cheese mixture for a fruitier taste. Pecans can be added to the icing. Cherries can decorate the top for a more festive look.

CHAPATI
Category: bread
Prep 1 hr | Cook 20 min | Total 1 hr 20 min | Serves 6 | Author: Imma

Ingredients:
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 3 tablespoons ghee (clarified butter or oil)
- 1 1/4 cups warm water

Directions (condensed):
1. Mix flour, sugar and salt in a large bowl, then add the water and ghee/oil.
2. Knead into a soft, sticky dough, then knead on a well-floured board 8-14 minutes, adding flour as needed, until soft, elastic and smooth. Don't overdo it.
3. Divide into 6-8 pieces and let rest (relaxes the gluten so they're tender and easier to roll).
4. Roll each ball into a circle. (You can cook them now, but for layers keep going.)
5. Lightly oil the circle with ghee, lay it on a floured plate and press down, flip and press again, then shake off extra flour.
6. Fold it like a paper fan from one end to the other, then coil it up.
7. Cover the coils with a damp cloth and rest 20 minutes.
8. Gently flatten each coil and roll out from the center, rotating as you go, to about 1/4 inch thick (chapatis aren't meant to be thin).
9. Oil a crepe pan or skillet, cook the chapati, oiling the other side and the edges well. Cook 2-3 minutes, turning as needed, until golden brown. Serve warm.

Notes: Kneading is crucial for texture; oil keeps it soft. Lukewarm water gives a smoother dough. Don't overcook — a non-stick pan helps.

Lemon curd:
- 1 cup (230 g) lemon juice (fresh)
- Zest of one lemon
- 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 3 eggs
- 3 egg yolks
- 4 tbsp (56.5 g) unsalted butter

Coconut buttercream:
- 2 cups (452 g) unsalted butter, slightly cold
- 1/4 cup (57.7 g) cream of coconut (from the bottle, not the can)
- 6 cups (750 g) powdered sugar, measured then sifted
- 1 tbsp (16 g) coconut emulsion
- Pinch of salt
(Makes enough to fill and cover; make an extra 1/4-1/2 batch for extra piping.)

Whipped raspberry filling:
- 1/2 cup (15 g) freeze-dried raspberries
- 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 2 cups (455 g) heavy whipping cream

Directions (condensed):
CAKE: Preheat oven to 325°F. Spray three 8-inch pans, line with parchment, spray again. Whisk cake flour, baking powder and salt. In a stand mixer with paddle, cream butter and sugar on medium-high 3 minutes until light and fluffy. Gradually add egg whites and whole eggs, scraping the bowl; beat 2-3 more minutes. Add coconut emulsion and vanilla. On low, alternately add flour mixture and coconut milk, starting and ending with flour; mix until combined. Divide among pans. Bake about 23-25 minutes, until a toothpick has a few moist crumbs. Cool in pans 15 minutes, then invert onto racks. (Layers are 1-1.25 in tall; make 1.5x for thicker layers.)
LEMON CURD (best made a day ahead): Whisk sugar, lemon juice and zest in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Whisk eggs and yolks in a small bowl, then whisk into the lemon mixture. Cook, whisking constantly, until thick like pudding, 6-8 minutes. Off heat, whisk in butter a few pieces at a time. Strain through a fine sieve, press plastic wrap on the surface, and chill until set, at least 4 hours (up to 5 days).
BUTTERCREAM: Beat butter and cream of coconut on medium-high 1-2 minutes. On low, gradually add sifted powdered sugar, then coconut emulsion and salt. Beat on medium-high 3-5 minutes until light and fluffy. Stir with a wooden spoon to remove air bubbles.
RASPBERRY FILLING: Pulse freeze-dried raspberries to a powder in a food processor. Add sugar and cream, scrape down, and pulse 1-2 minutes until thick like Greek yogurt. Keep chilled.
ASSEMBLY: Place first layer top-side up; spread with about 1/2 cup buttercream and pipe a rim of buttercream around the edge. Spread about 1/2 cup raspberry filling, then about 1/4 cup lemon curd. Repeat for the second layer; top with the final layer bottom-side up. Crumb coat and freeze 10-15 minutes, then finish frosting and decorating.

SALTED CARAMEL (macaron filling) — "French Macarons for Beginners," p. 130
Category: dessert
Prep 45 min plus 1-2 hours setting time | Yield: filling for 12 macarons

Ingredients:
- 125 g (1/2 cup) heavy cream
- 100 g (1/2 cup) granulated sugar
- 30 g (2 tablespoons) water
- 113 g (1/2 cup; 1 stick) unsalted butter, cubed and softened
- 1.5 g (1/4 teaspoon) sea salt

Directions:
1. Warm the cream in the microwave about 20 seconds (makes it easier to incorporate into the hot sugar).
2. Pour the sugar into the center of a medium saucepan, then add the water, swishing it around to dampen the sides and toward the center to liquify the sugar. Gently swirl to add water evenly, but don't stir. Heat on medium-high; bring to a boil without stirring (stirring can make crystals form). When the syrup turns light golden amber, remove from the heat.
3. Gradually pour the cream into the syrup while stirring (it may bubble up). Return to medium heat, stirring continuously with a wooden spoon (ideally one with a straight edge) to blend and prevent scorching. It's normal for the sugar to seize after adding the cream — keep stirring over the heat and it will dissolve into a smooth sauce.
4. While cooking over medium heat, add the butter and swirl it in. Reduce heat to low and keep cooking, stirring, until thickened and golden, about 30 minutes — you want a filling that somewhat holds its shape.
5. Remove from heat and stir in the salt. Keep stirring to help it cool down.
6. Once cool enough to work with, transfer to a pastry bag (no tip needed). Squeeze a half-dollar-sized dollop onto the middle of a macaron shell.
7. Top with a matching shell, gently press and twist. Repeat.

- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1 1/2 tsp garam masala

PASTEL DE TRES LECHES (Tres Leches Cake)
Category: dessert
Serves 10 generous portions

Cake:
- 1 cup sugar (divided: 3/4 cup + 1/4 cup)
- 5 large eggs, separated
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp cream of tartar

Milk syrup:
- 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
- 1 cup sweetened condensed milk
- 1 cup heavy (whipping) cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 Tbsp dark Cuban rum

Garnishes:
- Fresh whipped cream or good-quality vanilla ice cream
- Cocoa powder
- Sliced fresh mango (or fruit of your choice — tropical fruits pair nicely)

Directions:
Cake: Preheat oven to 350°F. Generously butter a 13x9-inch baking dish. Beat 3/4 cup sugar and the egg yolks until light and fluffy, about 5 minutes. Fold in the milk, vanilla, flour and baking powder. Beat the egg whites to soft peaks, adding the cream of tartar after 20 seconds. Milk syrup: Combine the evaporated milk, sweetened condensed milk, cream, vanilla and rum in a mixing bowl. Whisk until well blended. Pour the syrup over the cake, spooning the overflow back on top, until it is all absorbed.
To serve: Cut a slice and plate it. Top with a dollop of freshly whipped cream or a side of ice cream, dust with some cocoa powder and place a slice or two of fresh mango on the side. This cake is addictive — you've been warned!

JASMINE SYRIAN FOOD'S BAKLAVA (magazine clipping)
Category: dessert
Active 35 min | Total 2 hrs 10 min | Makes 72 pieces
To make ahead: Refrigerate airtight up to 5 days or freeze up to 4 months.
To make clarified butter: melt butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat, skim the milk solids from the top, and slowly pour the butter out, leaving the remaining solids in the bottom.

Ingredients:
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 3 teaspoons orange blossom water, divided
- 3/4 cup superfine sugar
- 6 cups chopped walnuts
- 18 (13x18-inch) sheets or 36 (9x14-inch) sheets phyllo dough (1 pound), thawed according to package directions
- 1 1/4 cups clarified butter or ghee, warmed
- 3 tablespoons unsalted pistachios, lightly toasted and minced

Directions:
1. Combine granulated sugar and water in a medium saucepan; bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring occasionally. Stir in lemon juice. Reduce heat to maintain a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until reduced to a scant 1 3/4 cups, 15 to 18 minutes. Remove from heat and stir in 1 teaspoon orange blossom water. Let cool for 10 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, preheat oven to 350°F. Coat a large rimmed baking sheet with cooking spray.
3. Combine 1/3 cup of the syrup, superfine sugar and the remaining 2 teaspoons orange blossom water in a large bowl. Stir in walnuts.
4. Unroll phyllo (keep the stack covered with a damp kitchen towel while you work). Lay 1 sheet in the prepared pan and brush generously with butter (or ghee), making sure to cover all the dough. Repeat until there are 14 layers. (If using 9x14-inch sheets, use 2 sheets, slightly overlapping, for each layer.)
5. Spoon the walnut filling evenly over the phyllo. Press the mixture all over with the back of a spoon. Lay 1 sheet of phyllo on top and press it firmly with your palms. Brush generously with butter (or ghee). Repeat layering, pressing and buttering with the remaining 3 (or 6) phyllo sheets.
6. Using a sharp knife, trim any shaggy edges so they are flush with the sides of the pan. Using the knife and a ruler, score the phyllo at opposite long ends, marking six 2-inch strips. Cut along score lines, using the ruler as a guide. Then score on a diagonal, marking twelve 1 1/2-inch-wide strips to create diamonds. Using the ruler as a guide, cut along the score lines.
7. Bake the baklava, rotating the pan twice, until the phyllo is deep golden and crisp, including down into the cuts, 35 to 45 minutes. Immediately spoon the reserved syrup over the hot baklava. (If the baklava has cooled or the syrup is hot, it will be soggy.)
8. Sprinkle the center of each diamond with pistachios. Let cool in the pan on a wire rack for at least 1 hour. Re-cut, if needed, before serving.

CZECH POPPY SEED KOLACE (pronounced "ko-la-chay") (magazine clipping)
Category: dessert
Active 1 hr 5 min | Total 2 3/4 hrs | Makes 56 kolace
To make ahead: Freeze cooled kolace airtight for up to 1 month.
Equipment: Parchment paper
Tip: For the signature even indentation, use a 100-gram weight about 1 1/4 inches in diameter — or anything round and a touch smaller than the dough balls, like the bottom of a glass spice jar. Dust it with flour first to prevent sticking.

Dough:
- 1 1/4 cups low-fat milk
- 2 (1/4-ounce) packages active dry yeast (1 1/2 tablespoons)
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 3 large egg yolks, room temperature, plus 1 large egg mixed with 1 tablespoon water for egg wash, divided
- 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick) unsalted butter, melted and cooled to room temperature
- 1/4 cup canola oil
- 2 cups white whole-wheat flour
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup confectioners' sugar
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1/3 cup lard, melted and cooled to room temperature

Filling:
- 1 cup low-fat milk
- 1 cup poppy seeds
- 2 teaspoons all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons honey
- Zest of 1 lemon

Directions:
1. To prepare dough: Heat 1 1/4 cups milk in a small saucepan over medium-high heat until steaming. Let cool until about 110°F. Stir in yeast and granulated sugar. Set aside until frothy, about 10 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, place egg yolks in a medium bowl. While whisking the yolks, gently stream in melted butter and oil.
3. Combine whole-wheat flour, 2 cups all-purpose flour, confectioners' sugar, salt and lemon zest in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook or in a large bowl. Add the yeast and yolk mixtures, then beat until the dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl and forms a ball, 3 to 5 minutes with a stand mixer or 10 to 15 minutes by hand.
4. Slowly add lard and mix until all the lard is incorporated and the dough no longer sticks to the bowl, 3 to 5 minutes. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let the dough rise in a warm place until doubled in size, about 1 hour.
5. Meanwhile, prepare filling: Mix milk, poppy seeds, flour, honey and lemon zest in a small saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until bubbly and thickened, 8 to 10 minutes. Remove from heat and let cool completely, about 1 hour.
6. Line 3 baking sheets with parchment paper. Turn the dough out onto a floured surface. Use a knife to slice off walnut-size chunks. Flatten each with your palm, then fold the edges under to create a tight ball. Arrange the balls, seam-side down, on the prepared baking sheets, about 2 inches apart. Cover with plastic wrap and let double in size, 30 to 40 minutes.
7. Preheat oven to 375°F.
8. Dust the bottom of a small spice jar (or other similar-size object) with flour and use it to press a large indentation into the middle of each kolace, leaving about a 1/4-inch lip. Brush with egg wash. Fill each indentation with the filling.
9. Bake the kolace until puffy and lightly golden, 10 to 12 minutes. Transfer to a wire rack to cool, about 10 minutes.

LOCRO DE PAPAS (Potato & Peppers Soup) (magazine clipping)
Category: soup
Active 35 min | Total 55 min | Serves 6 (1 1/2 cups each)
To make ahead: Refrigerate roasted peppers (Steps 1-2) up to 2 days.
A thick, hearty Ecuadorian potato soup. Seek out achiote paste — there's no good substitute.

Ingredients:
- 2 large red bell peppers
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/3 cup minced shallot
- 2 large cloves garlic, minced
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon achiote paste
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth, divided
- 2 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cubed (5 cups)
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground pepper
- 1 cup crumbled queso fresco
- 1 ripe avocado, diced
- 1/4 cup minced fresh cilantro

Directions:
1. Position rack in upper third of oven; preheat broiler to high.
2. Place bell peppers on a baking sheet. Broil, turning every 5 minutes, until charred and softened, 15 to 20 minutes. Transfer to a bowl, cover with plastic wrap and let stand for 10 minutes. Peel, seed and chop the peppers; set aside.
3. Meanwhile, melt butter in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add shallot, garlic and cumin; cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add achiote paste and 1 cup broth; cook, stirring and breaking up the achiote, until it's dissolved. Add potatoes and the remaining 3 cups broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to maintain a simmer, cover and cook until the potatoes are easily pierced with a fork, 15 to 18 minutes.
4. Puree the soup with an immersion blender or in a regular blender (in batches, if necessary) until very smooth. (Use caution when pureeing hot liquids.)
5. Add the reserved peppers, milk, salt and pepper to the soup. Bring to a simmer over medium heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until heated through, 5 to 8 minutes. Serve topped with cheese, avocado and cilantro.

CANDIED PECANS
Category: snack
Recipe by Joyce Hanlon

Ingredients:
- 2 cups sugar
- 1/2 cup boiling water
- 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg or cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon cream of tartar
- 4 cups pecan halves

Directions:
Generously butter a cookie sheet. Combine the sugar, water, nutmeg or cinnamon, and cream of tartar in a 4-6 quart saucepan. On medium-high heat cook, stirring till sugar dissolves. Bring the syrup to a boil and let boil covered 3 more minutes. Uncover the pan. Dip a pastry brush in water and wash down the sides of the pan. Cook the syrup over high heat without stirring, but swirling the pan by the handle, for 2-3 minutes or just till syrup turns the color of lightly brewed tea. Scatter pecans over the syrup and stir quickly with wooden spoon. Cook the syrup 2-3 minutes longer or till it is a golden brown color. Immediately pour nuts and syrup onto the baking pan, working quickly; separate the pecans with a fork. Set the pan on a wire rack and cool the candy until hard, at least an hour, then break into pieces.

CHOCOLATE AND PEANUT BUTTER POPCORN (Lindt)
Category: snack
Total 45 minutes

Ingredients:
- 3 Tbsp (45 ml) vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup (125 ml) corn kernels (10 cups popped popcorn)
- 1/2 cup (125 ml) peanuts
- 2 sticks (225 g) butter
- 2 cups (500 ml) dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (125 ml) light corn syrup
- 1/2 cup (125 ml) creamy peanut butter
- 1/2 tsp (2 ml) baking soda
- 1 tsp (4 ml) vanilla extract
- 1 tsp (4 ml) salt
- 1 bar (100 g) Lindt Excellence A Touch of Sea Salt dark chocolate

Directions:
1. Preheat oven to 250°F (120°C) and line a baking tray with parchment paper. Set aside.
2. Pop the corn: In a medium pot, heat the oil and a few kernels over medium-high heat. When they pop, add the rest of the kernels and swirl to coat. Once corn starts popping, turn heat down to medium-low and cover with a tight-fitting lid. Shake the pan continuously over heat until popping slows. Remove from heat when popping stops, pour into a large mixing bowl, add the peanuts and set aside.
3. Wipe out the pot and return it to medium-high heat. Add butter, brown sugar, corn syrup and peanut butter; stir well and bring to a boil. Turn heat down slightly and keep boiling, stirring often to prevent burning. After 7 minutes, remove from heat and add baking soda, vanilla and salt — the caramel will bubble up, so be careful.
4. Immediately pour caramel over the popcorn and stir to coat. Spread on the baking sheet and bake, stirring every 15 minutes, about 45 minutes total, separating the kernels as you go. Remove and let cool.
5. Melt the chocolate in a small heatproof bowl in the microwave in 15-second increments (or in a bain-marie) until just melted. Once the popcorn is cool and set, pour the chocolate over and toss to coat. Let harden before serving.
